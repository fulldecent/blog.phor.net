## Different tone, could have same meaning

Here are some words/phrases where if you change the tone of the words then you will have another word which could have the same/similar meaning in some circumstance.

* 今 jīn (this day/year)／近 jìn (recent days/years)

  Both time periods include the current day/month/year (e.g. 今天), but the latter includes some periods prior (e.g. 近天).

* 接收 jīeshōu (receive) / 接受 jīeshòu (accept)

  Both are a response to sending something. The former is the physical collection of the thing at the destination. The latter is the emotional approval of the thing at the destination.

* 陪 péi (accompany) / 配 pèi (compliment [v])

  Both describe a relationship between two things which are together. The former explains their physical proximity. The latter describes their fitness to be together.

* 先去 xīanqù (go first)／现去 xìanqù (go now)

  Both describe the timing of an event, usually an event that is iminent, and which is being negotiated with others. The former can explain that you will go first, to the exclusion of somebody else, perhaps in a queue (e.g. 我先去). The latter implies the former—there is only one now, so if you are doing something now then another person is not (e.g. 我现去).

* 找人 zhǎorén (seek somebody)／招人 zhāorén (seek somebody, to hire them)

  Both talk about looking for a person. But the latter involves looking for a person if your intent is to employ them. In other words, you are recruiting them.

* 美眉 měiméi ("crush", cutie)／妹妹 mèimèi ("little sister", cutie)

  These two phrases share the same meaning, but both also have other meanings. They both refer to a girl that is attractive, not older than the people that are talking, and a possible target to engage courting.

* 调 tiáo (modify) / 挑 tiāo (select)

  There is probably an argument that these two words semantically describe the same thing. But circumstances dictate when you would use one word or the other. Air conditioning, changes the air (空调) but a picky eater is selecting food (挑食).

* 加人 jīarén (join people) / 嫁人 jìarén (marry somebody)

  The former former can be used to talk about going with a person or group of people, maybe to play along in some activity. The latter is more permanent.

* 好神奇 hǎoshénqí (amazing) /  好神器 hǎoshénqì (amazing tool)

  The former is a facinating or amazing event or circumstance. Maybe it is a coincidence or irony. The latter is a facinating tool, that is purpose-made to do some super-specific task, like peel the rind off a lemon. A lot of kitchen tools are 神器. But if you are watching the operation of a 好神器, you may say it is 好神奇.
