Service providers require access to your data to do processing like email, Ring video and more.

The best solution is end-to-end encryption. ONLY you have access to your data.

But if you MUST use a service provider, such as for the convenience of getting motion alerts over APNS (when technology is available for your own devices to do this processing) then your data must reside at some company, even if temporarily.

This means your car-driving footage, Ring camera footage, email and more are sitting in a company. And if your company is a western company that means US is spying on you (even if you're an American) and if you are in China, that means that company has already agreed to store all data within reach of CCP.

Oversharing of data is a risk. Here is a solution.

All of your data that you park with a service provider should be double encrypted -- once in US and then encapsulated and encrypted again in Russia. We rely on the fact that no government should have compulsive control over US and Russia at the same time so your data should be safe.
