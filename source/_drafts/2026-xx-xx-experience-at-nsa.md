Twenty years ago I was inside the National Security Agency (NSA) at Fort Meade, Maryland and saw something disturbing. I'm sharing it years later because the pieces finally fit.

First, proof that I was actually there. . LINK TO OTHER POST.

I won't tell you my exact job title. But do know that my senior project in college was to do data mining of all data on Thefacebook and public webpages about my college and use it to infer and classify relationship networks among people. I also contributed to network stack code used in the Linux kernel.

When I went up to the room to do my interview on a specific team, the main door we entered had a light switch next to it. And the light switch caused some flashing red light to come on and a moment later all monitors went dark and people peek up to get a look at me. But that was enough time to give me a clue: a glimpse of what was on the monitors and the faces in the room.

This was 2005. Which is after the 2001 9/11 attacks, and before lots of servicemen came back from the middle east, and well before the Snowden releases.

In the morning I came in on a bus with other candidates. And at the lunch hall, I was able to see other people shuffling through from other departments.

Lots of faces. But one problem.

Based on the mission as I knew it, there were too many white-skinned people on base and too many black-skinned people.

The mission involved language skills and foreign intelligence after 9/11. But I didn't see any brown-skinned people.

This made me uneasy for years, was the mission a farce? Years later, of course we all now know why.
